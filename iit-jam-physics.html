Exam Pattern

IIT JAM Physics Exam Pattern 2026

IIT JAM Physics is a 3-hour exam with 60 questions across 3 sections, assessing conceptual understanding and problem-solving ability.

Section A 30 MCQ Questions

1 or 2 marks each. Negative marking of 1/3 for 1-mark and 2/3 for 2-mark questions.

Section B 10 MSQ Questions

Multiple Select Questions — 2 marks each. No negative marking. All correct options must be chosen.

Section C 20 NAT Questions

Numerical Answer Type — 1 or 2 marks. No negative marking. Answer is a real number.

100 Marks Total in 3 Hours

Computer-based test (CBT). No physical calculators allowed.

Full Syllabus

IIT JAM Physics Syllabus — Topic-wise

Pravegaa's IIT JAM course covers every topic in the official JAM Physics syllabus with conceptual depth and PYQ-focused practice.

Mathematical Methods

  • Calculus of single & multi variables
  • Vector algebra & calculus
  • Linear algebra & matrices
  • Differential equations (ODE, PDE)
  • Fourier series & transforms
  • Complex analysis

Classical Mechanics

  • Newton's laws, conservation laws
  • Systems of particles, rigid body
  • Central force & Kepler problem
  • Non-inertial frames
  • Special theory of relativity
  • Lagrangian formulation

Oscillations, Waves & Optics

  • SHM, damped & forced oscillations
  • Resonance phenomena
  • Superposition, wave equation
  • Group & phase velocity
  • Interference & diffraction
  • Polarization, resolving power

Electricity & Magnetism

  • Gauss's law, Laplace & Poisson equations
  • Magnetostatics, Biot-Savart law
  • Electromagnetic induction
  • Maxwell's equations
  • EM waves in vacuum & media
  • AC circuits, LCR circuits

Kinetic Theory & Thermodynamics

  • Kinetic theory of gases
  • Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution
  • Laws of thermodynamics
  • Thermodynamic potentials
  • Carnot cycle & entropy
  • Phase transitions, van der Waals gas

Modern Physics

  • Blackbody radiation, photoelectric effect
  • Bohr model, hydrogen atom
  • Wave-particle duality, de Broglie
  • Heisenberg uncertainty principle
  • Schrödinger equation (1D)
  • Radioactivity & nuclear reactions

Solid State Physics

  • Crystal structures & Bravais lattice
  • X-ray diffraction & Bragg's law
  • Free electron theory
  • Band theory of solids
  • Semiconductors (intrinsic & extrinsic)
  • Magnetism: dia, para, ferromagnetic

Electronics

  • Semiconductor diodes & applications
  • Transistor characteristics (BJT)
  • Amplifiers & oscillators
  • Operational amplifiers
  • Boolean algebra & logic gates
  • A/D and D/A converters
