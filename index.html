CSIR NET Physics Syllabus

CSIR NET Physical Sciences — Topic Weightage Analysis

Based on previous year question paper analysis. Prioritize high-weightage topics for maximum marks improvement.

Topic / Unit Key Subtopics Weightage Priority
Quantum Mechanics Schrödinger equation, perturbation theory, angular momentum, identical particles, scattering ~18–22% 🔴 Highest
Classical Mechanics Lagrangian & Hamiltonian mechanics, central force, rigid body, special relativity ~12–16% 🔴 Highest
Electromagnetic Theory Maxwell's equations, wave propagation, radiation, boundary conditions, multipoles ~14–18% 🟡 High
Statistical Mechanics Ensembles, partition function, quantum statistics, phase transitions, fluctuations ~12–14% 🟡 High
Mathematical Physics Complex analysis, Green's functions, tensors, group theory, special functions ~10–12% 🟡 High
Nuclear & Particle Physics Nuclear models, radioactivity, particle classification, quark model, detectors ~8–10% 🟢 Medium
Condensed Matter Physics Crystal structure, band theory, magnetism, superconductivity, semiconductors ~8–10% 🟢 Medium
Electronics & Atomic/Molecular Diodes, transistors, op-amps, spectroscopy, selection rules, lasers ~6–8% 🟢 Medium

* Weightage is approximate based on CSIR NET PYQ trend analysis. Actual distribution may vary per session.
